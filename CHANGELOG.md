# Timepiece changelog

## v0.7.0

- Snoozed alarms show in the app: a Snoozed card at the top of Alarms with the time it rings again and Dismiss, a note on the alarm itself, and a quiet notification with Dismiss
- New timer is quicker: scroll the hours, minutes and seconds like wheels, or tap a number to type it. It starts at 0, and +1m, +5m, +10m and +30m add to what is set
- The background gears race and a fast hand sweeps the clock while the stopwatch runs, every lap flashes the dial, and a running timer shows as a ring round the clock that shrinks as time runs out
- The background answers your finger: a tap sends rings out and pops what it lands on, and dragging or scrolling leaves a wake and stirs the scene. Saving, adding, deleting and finishing things send a wave of colour through it, and an error gives a red shudder
- Alarms can have a note: it shows under the time in the alarm list, in the ringing notification, on the full screen ring and in the upcoming alarm notice, and editing an alarm updates that notice straight away
- The animated background now has a picture of its own behind everything: turning gears behind a faint clock that tells the real time. It is faint, so text stays easy to read
- Aqua changes far more through the day: a rose dawn, gold sunrise, turquoise morning, deep azure noon, warm afternoon, orange sunset, violet dusk and a real dark night with the moon shimmering through the water and glowing plankton. Now and then a cloud passes over and the light dims
- Super's space changes with the time of day too: a dark night with brighter stars, a rose dawn, teal morning, cobalt noon and a red and amber sunset
- Opening the app no longer sprays a burst of fast symbols across the background; celebrations are a gentle lift of a few symbols, and only for something you just did

## v0.6.0

- The background reacts to your timers: calm blue while there is plenty of time, turning through purple and pink to red as the nearest timer runs out, quicker and breathing in the last seconds, and a red flash when one finishes
- In the last hour before your next alarm the Alarms background warms like a sunrise; at night with an alarm set it goes a quiet midnight blue with moons and stars
- A running stopwatch races along in green; a paused one holds still

## v0.5.29

- The symbols drifting in the background are smaller and fainter so they no longer crowd text; selected segments and chips are readable in every look (including Super); the warning banner at the top has a solid backing so its text is easy to read

## v0.5.28

- The animated background is now drawn directly on the screen at full sharpness instead of a small picture stretched up, so it is smooth and no longer looks pixelated; the grain over it is much subtler, and long tab names (like Feedback) shrink to fit instead of being cut off

## v0.5.27

- At night the animated background gets a stronger wash, so text stays easy to read on dark scenes

## v0.5.26

- Each app's animated scene now has its own drifting symbols (maths signs in Abacus, notes in Echo, arrows in Wayfinder and so on), and the scene is a little clearer while text stays easy to read
- Animated backgrounds reworked after your report: they are drawn at a higher resolution and smoothly at 24 frames a second without slowing the app down, and no longer show horizontal lines

## v0.5.25

- Every app window now has the animated scene behind it, in the app's own colour (for example orange water in Abacus, green in Pulse, purple in Scribble), following your Aqua, Bliss or Super look and the real sunrise and sunset. Motion Reduced shows a still picture and Off turns it off

## v0.5.24

- Fixed a gap above the keyboard in screens with a bottom tab bar: the tab bar now steps aside while you type, so you can see what you are writing

## v0.5.23

- Settings are grouped under clear headings: new alarms, clock, while an alarm rings, and more

## v0.5.22

- New settings for what a new alarm starts with: time, vibration, gentle volume, snooze length and a sum to turn off

## v0.5.21

- New setting: show or hide seconds on the clock

## v0.5.20

- New setting: show the clock in 12 or 24 hours whatever the phone uses

## v0.5.19

- Text size and accent colour chosen in Control Room now apply here
- New Rotate with the phone switch in Settings

## v0.5.18

- New alarm settings: how long an alarm rings, auto snooze, loudest volume, gentle wake-up length and vibration pattern

## v0.5.17

- Loading screens now say what they are doing instead of showing a bare spinner
- Wording tidied so no sentence needs a dash

## v0.5.16

- Alarm sounds made by Timepiece: Aqua Drop, Glass Bell, Morning Chime, Sunrise and Digital Pulse, with a preview when you tap (alarm editor › Wonderdesk sounds)
- Snooze can be switched off per alarm (Off next to 5, 10, 15, 20): the ring screen and notification then have only Dismiss
- Weekly wake-up (Settings › Alarms): choose a different time for each day and Timepiece makes the repeating alarms for you

## v0.5.15

- Swiping a ringing alarm or timer notification away turns it off (alarms set to need a sum solved bring the ring screen back instead)
- Alarms now take the whole screen even while you're using the phone, once you allow "Display over other apps" (Settings → Permissions and access); before, Android shrank them to a small banner
- The ring screen closes by itself when the alarm is stopped from the notification
- Titles shrink to fit instead of being cut off

## v0.5.14

- New Timer buttons widget: start a timer in one tap from the home screen with four buttons you choose (1 minute to 2 hours); the next timer to finish counts down live on the widget
- Fixed a crash when moving around with arrow keys or Tab while a screen was closing

## v0.5.13

- Fixed: Android's “Alarms & reminders” switch for Timepiece was greyed out and stuck off on newer phones, so alarms could ring late. Timepiece now asks for the permission the switch controls, so you can turn it on, and the banner and Settings explain exactly what to tap

## v0.5.12

- Back up and restore alarms (Settings › Alarms): save every alarm to a file and bring them back after reinstalling or on a new phone; restoring skips alarms you already have

## v0.5.11

- A ringing alarm can always be stopped: if its notification is swiped away by accident it comes straight back with Dismiss and Snooze
- Opening Timepiece while an alarm or timer rings goes straight to the big Dismiss / Snooze screen
- Switching off or deleting an alarm while it rings stops it at once

## v0.5.10

- Bedtime reminder (Settings › Alarms): a gentle nudge at the time you pick, with your next alarm and how many hours of sleep you'd get if you went to bed now

## v0.5.9

- Lighter on the battery: in the Super look the drifting sparkles settle after a few seconds without a touch (the next tap wakes them), so a screen you're reading no longer redraws 25 times a second

## v0.5.8

- Changing the look in Control Room now restyles this app's widgets straight away, with no refresh or restart

## v0.5.7

- Widgets: choose the title colour in each widget's settings (automatic, white, dark, Aqua blue, Super pink or gold), on top of the background looks

## v0.5.6

- The “Upcoming alarm” notice no longer comes back after you swipe it away, or when you open the app
- A repeating alarm can no longer ring twice in the same minute

## v0.5.5

- Widgets can now match your look: new Bliss blue and Super neon backgrounds, and “Match my Wonderdesk look” follows whatever Control Room is set to

## v0.5.4

- Fixes from a full round of stress testing on every screen

## v0.5.3

- Alarms that Android might block are flagged at the top of Alarms: notifications off, ringing silenced, exact alarms or full-screen alerts not allowed, each one tap from the right switch
- New Settings row for full-screen alerts, which Android 14 and newer may not grant to apps from outside the Play Store
- Import from Clock: the greyed-out accessibility switch is now explained step by step (Android blocks it as a “Restricted setting” until you allow it in App info)
- Solve a sum to dismiss: an option per alarm for heavy sleepers
- The ringing screen follows your look
- Swipe an alarm right to turn it on or off, left to delete it, with Undo
- Tap the tab you're already on to glide back to the top of its list
- Nothing required stays hidden: if Android is blocking something the app needs, a slim banner says what and takes you straight to the switch, and Settings › Permissions and access lists everything with its state

## v0.5.2

- Alarms and timers glide into place when you add or remove them
- Smoother everywhere: new screens, tabs and Settings ease in instead of snapping, and buttons, tabs, chips and switches give a light tap you can feel (following your phone's touch-feedback setting)
- Android's predictive back: the back gesture shows a live preview of where you're going
- Clock widget fits any size, down to a 1×1 square
- Super look: tap sparkles now match Bliss Player: soft glowing sparks with trails, drifting rainbow colours and a faint aurora, instead of big solid circles

## v0.5.1

- Much faster and smoother: the app is now built optimised (the way Play Store apps are), so screens open quicker, scrolling is fluid and the APK is about a quarter of the size
- Screens and lists no longer decode pictures on the main thread, and skip redrawing parts that didn't change

## v0.5.0

- Clock widget settings: any city's time zone, seconds, date, next alarm, Timer and Alarms buttons, and the background
- Fixed a crash with the same city added twice
- Works properly in landscape: tabs move to a rail at the side so the content keeps its height, and screens that ran out of room now scroll
- If the app ever crashes it says so next time it opens and offers to send a report (only the error, app version and phone model, with file names and links removed). Turn on automatic sending in Control Room › Wonderdesk
- Lists can no longer crash when two items share a name

## v0.4.0

- New Clock widget showing the time, date and your next alarm, with Timer and Alarms buttons
- If the app ever crashes or freezes, a report goes to Control Room so it can be shared and fixed
- App icon art is larger and fills its tile without being cut off

## v0.3.1

- Bring alarms over from your phone's Clock app: import the next alarm with one tap, or all of them (times, labels, repeat days and on/off) by scrolling Clock once
- Google Assistant and other apps can set alarms and timers in Timepiece
- Clocks and the stopwatch pause completely in the background and run smoothly on screen
- App icon redrawn so nothing is cut off: round icons fill the space, the rest sit on an Aqua tile
- Smoother and lighter on the battery: the window background no longer animates, tap effects sleep until you tap, and nothing ticks while the app is in the background
- Opening the app no longer waits on Control Room to read the suite look

## v0.3.0

- Alarms that ring on the lock screen with your choice of sound
- Repeat patterns, Skip next and Pause for today
- Timers, multiple stopwatches and a world clock
